Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how user information is handled in the mobile application “Kokoro Diary (hereinafter referred to as “this Application”).

1. Collection and Purpose of Use

This Application collects and uses user information for the following purposes:

  • Advertisement delivery via Google AdMob
  • User authentication via Firebase Authentication (Note: passwords are not stored)
  • Storage of user-entered diary data in Cloud Firestore
  • Analysis and summarization of diary entries, chat and voice responses, and diary draft creation using OpenAI APIs
  • Aggregation of usage counts and diary creation modes to provide and improve features and prevent abuse

2. Types of Information Collected

  • Firebase UID (User Identifier)
  • Diary content, chat input, voice conversation audio and transcriptions, and photos attached to diaries
  • Diary creation modes, usage timestamps, and feature usage counts
  • Device information such as Advertising ID
  • App usage logs and access data (e.g., IP address, timestamps)

3. Third-Party Services

This Application uses the following third-party services. Each of these services may manage and use user information in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, App Check, etc.)
  • Google AdMob
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)

Data Sent to OpenAI for Chat and Voice Features

The chat feature sends your input and conversation history to OpenAI. The voice feature sends audio captured by your microphone to OpenAI to generate responses and transcriptions. To ask questions relevant to the conversation, the Application also sends context from your own recent diary entries (up to seven entries, including the first 1,000 characters of each entry and its date and time). Your statements from the conversation are sent to create a diary draft.

Audio is streamed during a voice conversation. In manual-send mode, the Send button starts the AI response; it does not mean that audio remains only on your device until you press it. When the microphone is paused, audio is not sent to the conversation. Ending the voice conversation stops audio transmission.

A diary created from a conversation is saved to Cloud Firestore, linked to your account, when you choose to save it. The conversation feature does not store audio recording files or full conversation transcripts in Firestore as a separate history. OpenAI handles and retains data according to its API data policies. By default, API data is not used to train models and may generally be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. See OpenAI's API data controls for details.

4. Opt-Out

Users can stop the collection of information by uninstalling this Application.

5. Data Retention

User data is retained during app usage and for a certain period afterward. To request deletion, please contact us at the email address below.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect collected information from leaks, alterations, and unauthorized access.

7. Children's Privacy

This Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. If it is discovered that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, it will be promptly deleted.
